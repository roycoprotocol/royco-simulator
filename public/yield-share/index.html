How these numbers update

They are not on a clock. Royco recomputes the Junior's yield share whenever the market is synced, and a sync happens on any interaction with the market: a Senior or Junior deposit, a redemption, or a yield accrual. Between two interactions the share is held as a time-weighted average of what it was, so it only moves when something actually happens.

So a new deposit is exactly one of the events that refreshes utilization and re-reads the share. It is lazy and event-driven, not per-block and not fixed-daily.

Re-rating speed across markets

The re-rating speed only applies to the self-adjusting curve. Today every launched Royco market uses a fixed curve, so none of them has a live re-rating speed yet. The speed is a design parameter; the only reference values that exist in the code are these:

ReferenceCurveRe-rating speed
Code defaultself-adjusting1.0 / yr

Pareto FalconX (example Day market)fixednot applicable

The honest answer: there is no per-market re-rating speed to report yet. When a market launches on the self-adjusting curve, its speed will be one per-market setting, most likely near the 1.0 / yr default.
