How much of the Senior's yield the Junior earns depends on how used the Junior's cushion is. This shows the curve, and how the Junior's share moves as utilization changes over time.

In one line: the more the market's safety cushion gets used, the bigger the slice of yield the Junior earns for taking that risk.

Illustrative model of Royco's Yield Distribution Mechanism (YDM). The curve maps utilization to the share of Senior yield paid to the Junior, through anchors at 0%, the 90% target, and 100%. The self-adjusting rule drifts the target share over time toward wherever utilization pushes it. Not investment advice.
