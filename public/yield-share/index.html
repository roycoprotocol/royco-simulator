Yield Share over Time

Set the values, press Play, and watch the Junior's yield share re-rate day by day.

How it works

Royco Dawn splits an asset into a protected Senior and a first-loss Junior. The Junior earns a share of the Senior's yield for taking the first losses.

  • That share is not fixed. It re-rates over time, like Morpho's adaptive rate, but tuning the yield split instead of a borrow rate.
  • Utilization above 90%: the share climbs to attract Junior capital. Below 90%: it falls.
  • The further from 90%, the faster it moves. Bounded 0.01% to 100%.

How these numbers update

Not on a clock. The share updates whenever the market is touched.

  • Recomputed on every sync, and a sync fires on any interaction: a Senior or Junior deposit, a redemption, or a yield accrual.
  • Between two interactions the share is held as a time-weighted average, so it only moves when something actually happens.
  • So a new deposit is one of the events that refreshes utilization and re-reads the share. Event-driven, not per-block or fixed-daily.

How fast the share re-rates

Fast, and the pace scales with distance from 90%.

  • Gentle near the 90% target, fast the further utilization drifts away. Same idea as Morpho's adaptive interest rate, applied to the yield split instead of a borrow rate.
  • Real speed, from the deployed contracts: up to 100 per year at the extremes of utilization. A shared constant across markets, not a per-market dial.
  • The target share is bounded between 0.01% and 100%.
  • Takeaway: a market a few points off 90% re-rates within days, not months.

What this view simplifies

Three simplifications. None change the direction or the pace.

  • The re-rating acts on the curve's target share; what the Junior actually earns is that plus a fixed premium for utilization above 90%. Deployed markets are flat below target, so below 90% the share here is exact; above 90% a small premium rides on top, largest near full utilization where the share is near its cap anyway.
  • The Forecast holds each market's utilization flat at today's level. Real utilization keeps moving.
  • Re-rating pauses while a market sits in a drawdown recovery (fixed-term) state.
